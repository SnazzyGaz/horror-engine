# Recipes

These are six patches, built step by step from the default sound (**Preset → Cold Cellar**). The values are the knob readouts. Each recipe says where to find the knob: the voice panel (pick the voice with `1`–`9`), the Macros, or **Hall & Master**. Use headphones. Most of these live low and quiet.

## 1. Trepidation

Long silences, a creaking string, the odd drip. Nothing happens for a while, and that is the point.

1. **Macros:** Dread 35%, Distance 30%.
2. **Hall & Master:** Hall Decay 12 s, Size 85%.
3. **I. E-bow string:** Pitch 41 Hz, Slack 80%, Drive 40%, Damping 50%. Set Hold on. The string now breathes on its own.
4. **II. Hurdy-gurdy:** Root 41 Hz, Crank 0.4 /s, Rosin 70%. On the Machine, circle the crank very slowly for a few seconds, then let go. Repeat it every 15–20 s.
5. **VI. Spring tank:** Decay 4 s, Tension 30%. Tap the tank softly (`Alt`-tap on the Surface) about once every ten seconds, never on a beat.
6. Loop the spring taps (`Space`, tap, tap, wait, tap, `Space`) and press `G` so the ghost spaces them for you.
7. Ride Dread from 35% to 60% over a minute. The wheel's fifth begins to sour.

## 2. Deep horror ambient

A floor that never resolves.

1. **Macros:** Pitch −12 st, Distance 40%, Dread 45%.
2. **Hall & Master:** Hall Decay 18 s, Damping 70%. Harmonizer: Shift −12 st, Feedback 45%, Return 80%.
3. **Drone bed** (`F3`). The wheel and string start on their own, and the Aura freezes under them after two seconds.
4. **III. Bowed metal:** Object Rod, Size 120 Hz, Harmonizer send 50%. Water: Water 40%, Slosh 20%.
5. Bow the waterphone rods on the Machine with long, slow strokes, one every few seconds. Loop two of them with **Free length** on.
6. **VIII. Aura:** Darkness 70%, Breathe 50%.
7. Every minute or so, drag the left superball across the thunder sheet once, slowly.

## 3. A bowed riser

Eight seconds of a rod climbing mode to mode, then a cut.

1. **III. Bowed metal:** Object Rod, Size 220 Hz, Pressure 30%, Bow speed 60%, Hall 50%.
2. **Hall & Master:** Harmonizer Shift −12 st, Feedback 35%. Set this voice's Harmonizer send to 35%.
3. On the Surface (`3`), drag back and forth steadily. Every two seconds, hold `Shift` for a moment, then let go. Each change of pressure knocks the bow onto another mode.
4. Partway through, press **Riser** (`F1`). It takes over the bow and the string, pushes the pitch up 11 semitones over eight seconds, and cuts.
5. For a softer ending, press `F1` again before the end. The machine glides back instead of cutting.

## 4. A glass shimmer pad

High, far away and steady, for underneath a quiet scene.

1. **IV. Glass:** Surface Wine glass, Contact Finger, Wetness 90%, Pressure 40%, Water in glass 30%, Hall 60%.
2. **Macros:** Distance 40%, Material 85%.
3. **VIII. Aura:** Shimmer 40%, Darkness 30%, Grain 600 ms, Density 20.
4. On the Machine, circle the wine-glass rim slowly with the wet finger. On the Surface (`4`), draw slow circles. Faster circles beat faster.
5. After a few seconds, freeze the Aura (`F`) and take your hand away. The shimmer stays.
6. For a second voice, add a little **V. Struck metal** Bell at 880 Hz, Decay 9 s, struck very softly (`Alt`) every 20 s.

## 5. A spring stinger

A hit that comes out of the walls rather than at you.

1. **Body & piezo:** Knock to spring 70%, Body 50%.
2. **VI. Spring tank:** Decay 6 s, Dispersion 80%, Tension 40%.
3. **V. Struck metal:** Object Body, Pitch 70 Hz, Spring send 40%.
4. **Hall & Master:** Hall Decay 10 s.
5. Let the room be quiet. Then press **Stinger** (`F2`): the last moment swells back reversed, and 800 ms later the hit lands with the spring tank. Or play it yourself: one hard knock (`Shift`-click) on the bare wood of the spine. The springs answer a beat later.
6. Follow it straight away with a single **In the walls** burst (`F5`, then `F5` again a few seconds later).

## 6. Heartbeat under dialogue

Something you feel more than hear.

1. **Macros:** Dread 20%, Distance 20%.
2. **Heartbeat** (`F4`): about 57 bpm at this Dread. Each beat ducks the string, the wheel and the Aura a little, so the bed pulses with it.
3. **II. Hurdy-gurdy:** Level −16 dB, Root 49 Hz, Hold on.
4. **Master:** Tilt −30% to keep it out of the voice range.
5. As the scene tightens, raise Dread slowly toward 60%. The heart speeds toward 70 bpm, and the wheel's fifth turns.

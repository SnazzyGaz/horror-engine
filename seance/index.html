        Horror Engine · Séance

        A side-tester of the plugin layout: the Surface is a spirit board. Same engine, same sound, same keys. By Garyck Arntzen (x.com/_mintydoge).

        The board

        • Pick a voice from the row of tokens (or keys 1–9), then drag on the board. The glass planchette follows your hand and the letter under it lights.
        • Across is x, up is y, exactly as on the old Surface; the corners name what they do for this voice. For pitched voices the brass rule along the top marks semitones (0 is the centre).
        • Pressure: a pen uses its own; with a mouse hold Shift for hard, Alt for soft, or roll the wheel over the board.
        • Yes fires the Stinger, No the Riser, Goodbye is Silence.

        Around it

        • Right column: the four Macros, the Moments (your own too: Record moment, play, Record moment again) and the Pinned knobs of the selected voice.
        • Bottom: Voice, Effects, Looper and Your sound in open a drawer over the board. The strip under them is the signal flow; click a part of it to open its drawer.
        • Knobs: drag up and down (Shift for fine), wheel, double-click to reset, right-click for MIDI learn. Or press MIDI learn at the top, then click a knob.
        • Presets: ‹ › step through them, Browse lists and stars them. Undo and redo cover knobs, presets and the looper.

        MIDI

        Notes play the selected voice; CC 20–23 move Dread, Distance, Material and Pitch. Moments: notes on channel 16 from C2 (36) fire Riser, Stinger, Drone bed, Heartbeat, In the walls, then your own; CC 24–43 fire them too. Learned CCs win over the fixed ones on their channel.
